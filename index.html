About Me

I am a researcher interested in reliable reasoning for foundation models, with a particular focus on reasoning verification, neuro-symbolic reasoning, and trustworthy AI. My work studies how to identify, verify, and improve failures in multi-step reasoning systems.

Research

My current research centers on making language-model reasoning more verifiable and reliable. I am especially interested in methods that combine formal or probabilistic reasoning with foundation models, and in diagnostic signals that reveal when and why multi-step reasoning fails.
